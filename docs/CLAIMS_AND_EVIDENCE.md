# Claims and evidence guardrail

This file controls what the public website may state.

## Allowed claims

### About the practice
- Castillo is an independent finance-transformation consulting practice.
- It is led by Gerson Castillo.
- It works across finance processes, systems, data, automation and AI.
- Engagements may include diagnosis, functional design, implementation leadership, development and adoption.
- Work can be executed with the client’s IT team or third-party providers.
- Azhum may be used where appropriate but is not mandatory.

### About Gerson
- 17 years of finance leadership experience.
- Former Deputy CFO / Subdirector Financiero of a large Spanish logistics group.
- Experience in multi-entity finance environments.
- Experience in financial reporting, controllership, R2R, P2P and analytical accounting.
- Led financial-software implementation for Iberia and LATAM at a consolidation SaaS company.
- Can design and build software solutions.
- MSc in Big Data, Data Science & AI.
- Founder and architect of Azhum.

### About current finance-transformation work
May state in anonymized form:
- current engagement with a leading Spanish transport/logistics group above €1bn revenue;
- finance-transformation work involving analytical accounting, P2P architecture, finance systems, reporting, automation and data-quality controls;
- functional architecture and delivery leadership with the client’s IT team executing builds where applicable.

Do not name the current client unless Gerson explicitly authorizes it for the specific public use.

### About AI
May state:
- Azhum includes an LLM / AI agent layer;
- Gerson has built and deployed AI-enabled financial software;
- Castillo offers AI transformation, AI agents, intelligent automation and advanced analytics as services;
- Castillo can identify, design and implement AI use cases.

## Claims NOT currently allowed
Do not state or imply:
- Castillo has already deployed AI agents for external consulting clients;
- multiple clients use Castillo’s agents;
- quantified savings from client AI deployments;
- a production “agent factory” exists across client organizations;
- external client ROI from AI unless documented and explicitly approved;
- Fortune 500 AI deployments;
- “market leader”, “leading consultancy”, “proven at scale” or similar superiority claims;
- team size beyond the truth;
- logos or names of clients without permission;
- fabricated testimonials;
- fabricated adoption, accuracy or automation percentages.

## Language guardrail
Avoid “we” if it implies a current team. Prefer:
- Castillo
- la práctica
- Gerson
- trabajo / diseño / desarrollo
- neutral constructions such as “se define”, “se implanta”, “se coordina”

If future staff join, update this file before changing public language.

## Evidence-first principle
If a claim cannot be mapped to:
1. documented professional experience,
2. a demonstrable product,
3. a real public credential,
4. a specifically approved client fact,

do not publish it.
