# Castillo v2 — Strategic brief

## 1. What the website is
The website is the commercial home of **Castillo · Transformación Financiera**, an independent finance-transformation practice led by Gerson Castillo.

It is not:
- a personal CV website,
- a developer portfolio,
- an AI-agency landing page,
- the Azhum product website,
- a fictional multi-person consultancy.

## 2. Core narrative
Castillo works in the gap between Finance and Technology.

Finance knows the business problem but often struggles to specify the system.
Technology can build systems but may lack finance-domain context.
Castillo connects both sides and can operate from diagnosis and functional design through implementation, validation and adoption.

## 3. Market position
The practice should be positioned broadly enough to cover the real current mandate:
- finance transformation,
- process redesign,
- functional architecture,
- finance systems,
- reporting,
- analytical accounting,
- integrations,
- implementation leadership.

At the same time, **Data & AI should be visibly prioritized as the new commercial growth wedge**:
- AI opportunity assessment
- AI agents
- intelligent automation
- predictive / advanced analytics
- AI governance
- productionization and adoption

The homepage therefore sells **Castillo first**, with a strong AI-forward section rather than redefining the entire brand as an AI shop.

## 4. Buyer
Primary:
- CFO
- Finance Director
- Controller
- Head of Finance Transformation
- CEO in mid-market firms

Secondary / co-buyers:
- CIO / CTO
- CDO
- Head of Data / AI
- Operations / Administration leadership

## 5. Buyer problems
Examples:
- reporting is slow, manual or inconsistent;
- finance depends on spreadsheets and manual reconciliation;
- systems do not communicate;
- finance requirements are lost between users, IT and vendors;
- processes are inefficient or poorly controlled;
- analytical accounting is incomplete or not reconciled;
- ERP / EPM / reporting initiatives lack functional ownership;
- AI opportunities exist but there is no disciplined way to identify, prioritize, build and govern them.

## 6. Service architecture

### A. Finance Transformation
- process diagnosis
- As-Is / To-Be
- R2R, P2P, O2C
- close, reconciliations, reporting
- analytical accounting
- business cases and roadmap

### B. Systems & Functional Architecture
- functional requirements
- process / system architecture
- data requirements
- integrations
- ERP / EPM / reporting tools
- vendor and IT coordination
- UAT, implementation, adoption

### C. Data & AI
- AI opportunity discovery
- AI agents and intelligent automation
- predictive and advanced analytics
- GenAI applied to finance workflows
- data preparation / quality needed for AI
- governance and measurement

### D. Delivery Direction
- project structuring
- workplan and milestones
- quality gates
- validation
- change / adoption
- directing internal IT or third parties when they execute the build

## 7. Commercial scope discipline
The site should show broad capability without implying unlimited “fractional transformation director” availability.

Preferred wording:
- project-based
- defined scope
- explicit deliverables
- clear calendar
- technology-agnostic
- build directly when justified; otherwise direct/coordinate the appropriate team or provider

## 8. Credibility architecture
Do not fabricate case studies.

Credibility should come from:
- Gerson’s finance leadership background;
- current independent finance-transformation work;
- prior financial-software implementation experience;
- direct software-building capability;
- Azhum as proprietary technology proof.

The public site can describe experience categories without presenting confidential client work as public case studies.

## 9. Azhum relationship
Azhum remains an independent product brand.

Suggested website language:
“Castillo trabaja con la tecnología adecuada para cada caso. Cuando encaja, puede incorporar Azhum, la plataforma de inteligencia financiera fundada por Gerson Castillo.”

Never imply Azhum is mandatory.

## 10. Content style
Use short, high-information sections.
Technology should appear as a means, never as the hero.
Avoid long framework/logo catalogs on the homepage.
A secondary “capabilities / technology” section or page may exist for technical buyers.

## 11. CTA
Avoid generic “Contáctanos”.
Preferred directions:
- “Hablemos de tu proyecto”
- “Evaluemos la oportunidad”
- “Cuéntame qué proceso quieres mejorar”

Forms should qualify:
- process / area
- main problem
- systems involved
- current manual work
- whether internal IT / provider exists
